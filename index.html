Common Questions

Is there a joining or admission fee?

Admission is free during our current offer. Monthly fees depend on the plan you choose.

Do you have separate timings for ladies?

Yes, we have ladies-only slots and a female trainer. Contact us for the latest timings.

I am a beginner. Can I join?

Absolutely. Every new member gets a free fitness assessment and a beginner plan.

Can I freeze my membership?

Yes, Pro and Elite members can freeze their plan for up to 30 days per year.

How can I pay?

Cash, bank transfer, JazzCash or Easypaisa. Online payment will be added soon.
